Getting across the city had become a gamble. Northline was built as an operator: the people who drive are employed here, and the fare is agreed before the wheels move.

Companies use the desk for guests and standing routes. Travelers use it when the day has a flight, a board meeting, or a last seating that cannot slip.

You can plan a trip on this site or by phone. The desk assigns a driver and a cabin. The status stays on your member desk, and you can cancel until an hour before departure.
